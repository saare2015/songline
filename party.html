🎮 Joining a game?

Scan the QR code on the host's phone, or paste the join link the host sent you.

Connect your Firebase project

One-time setup on the host's device (see the README). In the Firebase console, open Project settings → Your apps → Web app and copy the firebaseConfig block. Paste it here.

Players don't need to do this. The join link and QR code carry the settings to their phones.
